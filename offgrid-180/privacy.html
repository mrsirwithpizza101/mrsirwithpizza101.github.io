OFFGRID 180 privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026. This policy covers the OFFGRID 180 app (package com.offgrid180.app) published by Gorge Line Studio.

OFFGRID 180 does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your plan, inventory, logs, medical notes, waypoints and photos are stored only on your device. Backups you export are saved where you choose. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Erase all data, deletes them. We cannot see or recover this data.

Location

The app uses your device's GPS only while the Maps, Compass or SOS screens are open, to show your coordinates and waypoints. The app never sends your location to us or anyone else, except as described under the optional weather forecast below.

Optional weather forecast

Only if you tap the forecast button while connected to the internet, the app sends your approximate coordinates (rounded to about 100 m) to Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) over an encrypted connection to download a forecast. Nothing else is sent. Open-Meteo's own privacy policy applies to that request.

Camera, photos and files

Photos and map images you choose to attach stay on your device.

Permissions

  • Location — show your position, compass bearing and SOS coordinates.
  • Internet — optional forecast and checking for updates only.
  • Vibration — alerts and SOS signals.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the OFFGRID 180 Google Play page.
